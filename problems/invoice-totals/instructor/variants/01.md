# Invoice reconciliation debrief

The statement query aggregated line items and payments after joining both one-to-many relationships.
An invoice with three lines and two receipts produced six joined rows.
Each line then contributed twice and each payment three times.

Aggregate each relationship at its own invoice grain before combining the totals.
Correlated subqueries do this in one database round trip.
Another valid approach batches each set of totals and merges by invoice ID.
Using `SUM(DISTINCT amount)` drops separate entries that happen to have the same value.
Preserve zero totals for missing entries and the sign on customer credits.
